<h2>Android Kudzu Grandma</h2>

<p>What if the kudzu that strangles many places in the American South retook the plastic covered furniture in the formal living rooms of your childhood? What if your android grandma finally snapped because the arbitrary rules and pomp of traditional Southern culture were no longer being upheld? The kudzu creatures would rise up to devour the plastic furniture covers, the prized furniture, and everything else. </p>
