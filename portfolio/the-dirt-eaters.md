<h2>The Dirt Eaters</h2>

<p>Because sometimes mothers eat their children. This piece is my reflection on humans as animals and the tenuous, sometimes dangerous relationship between offspring and mothers.</p>

<p>Made with openFrameWorks and 3d models of real world objects.</p>
