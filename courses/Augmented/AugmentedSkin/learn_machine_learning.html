Machine-Learning Logic in the Floating-Skin App

1. Pixels become numbers

A computer image is a grid of numbers. Each pixel has red, green, and blue values. Here we often reduce those values to brightness.

128

2. Brightness becomes displacement

The app maps brightness to geometry: displacement = lerp(minDisplacement, maxDisplacement, brightness).

Black can move inward, white can move outward, and gray can stay near the original face.

3. A face model predicts landmarks

Face landmark models predict many point locations. Those predicted points become a mesh. In this project, the mesh is an artistic scaffold rather than an identity-recognition system.

4. Weights mix visual choices

A simple machine-learning idea is weighted combination: output = weightA × A + weightB × B + weightC × C. Texture mixing, region selection, and displacement are approachable ways to teach this visually.
