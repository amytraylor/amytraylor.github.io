### Part Four: ***Organizing, Planning, Visiting***
#### Instructions
We will place our artworks together on one large map of Albuquerque.
Using the provided materials, place all 3 of your artworks at their respective positions on the class map. For each placement, include your name, section #, and the name of the artwork.

Here is the link to place your pins!

#### [Map for Art Pins](http://u.osmfr.org/m/1362178/)

We will make teams to visit the art, based on availability and location of the art. Time permitting, we will visit one piece together today.

*Now make plans...* 
- Who is on your team?
- Do you have sufficient contact information?
- Which artworks will you visit and when?
- When will you visit?

Turn in on Canvas.
